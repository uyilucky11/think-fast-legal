THINK FAST Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

THINK FAST ("the game") is made by Lucky Uyi. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact us at [uyilucky11@gmail,com] with any question or request.

1.Playing without an account

You can play every game mode without signing in. Your scores, levels, statistics and settings are stored on your device.

2.Information we collect

Account identifiers: When online features are available, the game creates an anonymous guest account (a random ID) so it can back up your progress and show your scores on leaderboards. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your account ID and, if you share them, your name and email address. - **Game progress and scores.** Your levels, XP, statistics, best scores and daily challenge results are saved to your account. When you submit a score, we also receive the answers and timings of that game so our server can check that the score is genuine. - **Leaderboard name.** Leaderboards show your Google or Apple name, or a tag such as "Guest-AB12" or "Player-AB12". Other players can see this name and your best scores. - **Purchases.** If you buy Remove Ads, the app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) processes the payment. We receive a purchase token or transaction ID to confirm the purchase with the store. We never receive your card or payment details. - **Advertising.** The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states) the game asks for your consent first, and on iPhone and iPad it asks for permission to track. Ads are not shown to players who bought Remove Ads (except rewarded videos you choose to watch). - **App integrity.** We use Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity and Apple App Attest) to confirm requests come from the genuine game. This processes device and app information but does not identify you.

3. How we use information

To run the game's online features (cloud save, leaderboards, purchases), to prevent cheating and fraud, to show ads, and to keep the game working and secure. We do not sell your personal information.

4.Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, server functions, App Check), Google AdMob (ads) and the Google Play and Apple App Store billing systems. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies: - Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Your choices

- **Ad privacy.** Change your ad consent at any time in Settings > Ads > Ad privacy choices (where it applies), or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. On iPhone, you can turn off tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. - **Delete your account.** Settings > Account > Delete account permanently deletes your account, your cloud save and your leaderboard scores. Progress stored only on your device stays until you use Settings > Data > Reset progress or uninstall the game. - **Requests.** You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, at [uyilucky11@gmail.com].

6. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to honour your purchase and meet legal obligations.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at:
